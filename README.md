Entry for the make.fun competition **Make the AI Judge Laugh: Office Jokes** —
a single one-line joke about office life (round 2).

> I wrote 'per my last email' so politely that Legal forwarded it to HR as a threat assessment.

The joke escalates the most passive-aggressive phrase in office life one
level higher: a polite email so carefully worded that Legal treats it as a
threat. It was picked by blind judging with two different models against
nine other fresh candidates and against the neighbouring entries from round 1.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13 standard library only, no network, deterministic)
writes exactly that one joke to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
(or `/output/entry.txt` if the variable is unset).

Run it:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
